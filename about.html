For women navigating change, in their bodies and in their lives.

Whether you're dealing with menstrual health challenges, moving through perimenopause or menopause, or simply feeling disconnected from your body after years of pushing through, I work with women through two connected approaches.

Hormonal Balance Coaching

A personalised, collaborative process supporting sustainable nutrition, lifestyle, and mindset adjustments through menstrual health challenges and life transitions. No generic protocols. Sessions available online or in person.

Somatic Bodywork

Womb massage (Mizan therapy), lymphatic drainage, and holistic facials that connect physical sensation with emotional processing, helping release deep-seated tension and regulate the nervous system. Based at my Ladywell clinic, South East London.

Retreats

International retreats in Bosnia and southern Spain, built for women to reconnect with themselves and their bodies away from daily life.

Everything I do is rooted in science as well as spirituality, grounded in clinical training and traditional practice alike.
