Why I Do This

I built my own website.
Then everyone started asking me how.

Through my training courses and community, I kept meeting the same people: talented, trained, building something real on Instagram, but with no website to show for it.

The reason was almost always the same. Website builders like Squarespace and Wix charge every month, and when you're already paying for a booking system, a domain and everything else that goes into running your own practice, another monthly fee just doesn't make sense.

So I started building sites for them, using the same tools I used for my own: clean, beautiful, and hosted for free. Whether you're a facialist, a massage therapist, a yoga teacher, an artist, or launching your first side hustle, if you've been putting off your website because it felt too expensive, too technical or too overwhelming, I'm here to make it simple.
